## About CalcTwix

**CalcTwix** is a high-precision, next-generation Android calculator engineered for speed, clarity, and zero distractions. Built to go far beyond basic arithmetic, it brings together an everyday quick calculator, a full scientific workspace, an AI-powered camera math solver, and a system-wide floating overlay into one lightweight app.

## Features
- **AI Camera Math Solver:** Point your camera at handwritten or printed equations (fractions, roots, exponent towers, integrals, algebra) for instant step-by-step solutions.
- **Floating Calculator:** Resizeable, draggable overlay window that works smoothly over notes, PDFs, browsers, and lectures.
- **Simple & Scientific Modes:** Full trig, logs, powers, `DEG`/`RAD` toggle, and instant live expression preview.
- **More Calculators:** Live **Currency Converter**, **World Time Zones**, and specialized unit converters — with one-tap home-screen pin shortcuts.
- **Adaptive Themes & Dynamic Icon:** Auto, Light, and Dark modes with custom accent colors and a matching dynamic launcher icon.
- **100% Free Forever:** No ads, no subscriptions, no paywalled features.
